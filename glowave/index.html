Before you ask

Questions, answered.

Will it damage my walls? +
No. The adhesive is strong enough to stay up but designed to peel off clean. Renters and dorm dwellers use it all the time.
Do I need the app, or does the remote work on its own? +
Either works. The remote handles colors, brightness, and presets instantly. The app unlocks music sync, the AI Vibe Designer, custom scenes, scheduling, and millions of color combos.
What is the AI Vibe Designer? +
It's built into the free app. Type or say a mood — like "sunset gamer den" or "cozy rainy night" — and it generates a matching multi-color scene across your strip in seconds. No fiddling with sliders.
How long is one strip? +
Each kit is 16ft (about 5m) — enough for one wall, a bed frame, or a desk. Want a full room? The 2 and 3-strip bundles connect together.
Does it work with Alexa or Google Home? +
Yes. Pair it once in the app and control your lights with your voice — "set the room to movie mode," "turn the lights pink," and more.
Can I cut it to fit a shorter space? +
Yes. There are marked cut-lines along the strip, and corner connectors let you bend it neatly around any layout.
How fast does it ship? +
Orders placed today ship within 24 hours with free shipping. You'll get tracking by email the moment it's on the way.

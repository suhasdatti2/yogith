How fast can you really build my store?

Most stores are built and launch-ready within 48–72 hours after your onboarding call and brand details are submitted. Scale clients get priority 48-hour delivery.

Do I need any tech or design skills?

None at all. Novora is fully done-for-you. You share your idea; we handle the build, products, branding, copy, payments, and conversion setup.

Is this just another dropshipping course?

No. We don't sell courses. We build the actual store and marketing system for you, then optionally optimize it month over month.

Do I own the store and pay any monthly fees?

You own 100% of the store and all assets. The build is a one-time fee. Standard Shopify subscription and any apps you choose are billed by those platforms directly. Optimization is an optional monthly add-on.

What if I don't have a product or niche yet?

Perfect time to start. Our AI analyzes trending, profitable niches and recommends winning products — so you launch with data, not guesswork.

What's the guarantee?

We guarantee an on-time launch — if we miss the window, we keep working free until your store is live. Clear scope and milestone updates throughout.
